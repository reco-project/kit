# What's not yet included

Reco Kit films matches today, but it is a reference design in progress. A few pieces are still on the way:

- **Case** - the printable STL parts are in `hardware/case/`; print settings and the parametric STEP source are still to add.
- **Assembly** - exploded view, wiring, and photos (`hardware/assembly/`).
- **BOM** - exact vendor SKUs + a `bom.csv` (`hardware/bom/`).

Want to close one of these? See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [forum](https://forum.reco-project.org).
